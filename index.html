Alessio Ferrarini

I am a third-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at IMDEA Software. Feel free to contact me at alessio.ferrarini@imdea.org.

Research

My research focuses on programming language theory, type theory, refinement types, semantics, and formal verification. I contribute to the development of Liquid Haskell. I am very fortunate to be advised by Niki Vazou.
